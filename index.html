This week at a glance

  • How things are going. Conversations rose to 1,438 from 1,343 the week before, the first rise after five weekly drops, and cost $15.89 each, down from $16.29. Lead forms held at 341 against 344, at $67.01 each, up from $63.61.
  • What to look into. Appointments booked show 198 against 219 the week before, with 14 marked as showed. Twelve calendars were removed in the CRM, so the week before was recounted on the current list and now reads lower than the 228 shown last week.
  • What is next. We recommend reviewing the San Bernardino lead form ads, which cost $105.46 per form against $67.01 overall, and moving that budget toward its message ads, which cost $18.47 per conversation.
  • Creative ideas to consider. Every top ad uses the same Spanish pitch: a VIP appointment at 5 or more dealerships, with ITIN, own business, first buyer and all credit welcome. The Puente Hills video, 'Yes, help me get a car', brought 73 conversations at $11.34. The Kia video, 'Yes yes, I want a new car', brought 65 at $9.05, and the Mazda flexible video with the same headline 52 at $10.61. Worth testing that headline in Surf City, where conversations cost $23.51, the most of any dealership.
