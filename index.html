This week at a glance

  • How things are going. Appointments booked rose to 243 from 202 the week before, and 50 appointments showed up. Conversations held steady at 1,585 for $14.39 each, while lead form sign-ups slipped to 369 from 404.
  • What to look into. Surf City is the costliest region per conversation at $22.27 against $14.39 for the account overall, and San Bernardino has the highest cost per lead form at $93.65. Four of the new September ads were not approved in Meta's review; we recommend fixing and resubmitting any still blocked.
  • What is next. The new September videos went live across the dealerships between Sep 22 and Sep 26, so we recommend giving them a full week before judging them, then moving budget toward the dealerships with the lowest cost per conversation, Mazda and Kia first.
  • Creative ideas to consider. All top ads run one pitch. The Puente Hills video (78 conversations), headline 'Yes, help me get a car,' promises the best deals on cars and trucks, lists ITIN, self-employed, first buyers, all credit types, then asks for a message to book a VIP visit. Mazda and Kia videos use 'Yes yes, I want a new car': 75 and 66 conversations at $8.73 and $9.33. The work truck ad, 'Need a truck for work?', brought 61 at $9.05. We recommend testing the truck headline in Mazda and Kia, the cheapest regions.

Actions taken this week

  • New September videos launched across the dealerships: 47 new ads between Sep 22 and Sep 26 in new ad sets for Surf City (Sep 23), Puente Hills and San Bernardino (Sep 24) and Mazda (Sep 26).
  • Four of the new September message ads were not approved in Meta's review between Sep 23 and Sep 26; one was resubmitted on Sep 26.
  • Daily budgets trimmed on Sep 22 across the message campaigns: Corona $383 to $350, Surf City $473 to $462, Puente Hills $404 to $397, Audi $400 to $394, Mazda $277 to $267.
  • Lead form campaign budgets on Sep 22: Corona $67 to $60 a day; San Bernardino moved to $186.54 and then $90 a day (was $97). Three Corona ad sets cut from $15 and $11 to $10 a day.
  • Eleven older ads switched off on Sep 22 and 24, among them the Dodge video, the April Mazda video and the January, July and August control ads.
  • Two older ad sets turned back on: the March Mazda lead form set (Sep 22) and the July Audi April-videos set (Sep 24). A new control video ad was created Sep 22.
  • The May videos ad was edited and re-approved on Sep 23.
