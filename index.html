This week at a glance

  • How things are going. Conversations were 1,659 at $16.05 each, down from 1,834 at $14.62 the week before. Lead forms were 674 against 821, and appointments booked were 628 against 648 the week before.
  • What to look into. Oxnard conversations cost $22.64 each, the highest of any dealership, and none of its 64 booked appointments are marked as showed. Kia and Mazda also show no shows this week, so it is worth checking that their teams mark shows in the CRM.
  • What is next. We recommend testing a move of part of the Oxnard message budget into the Oxnard lead form campaign, where lead forms cost $16.47 each, since Oxnard conversations cost the most of any dealership.
  • Creative ideas to consider. The top ads are Spanish videos and posts selling a VIP appointment at several dealerships. The Mazda video, 'Yes yes, I want a new car', for all credit types, brought 84 conversations at $9.05 per result. The Puente Hills video, 'Yes, help me get a car', brought 79, and the same video with Surf City copy for ITIN and self-employed buyers brought 56. The Corona post for excellent credit brought 65 at $8.75. Worth testing the Puente Hills video with Oxnard copy, where conversations cost the most.
