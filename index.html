This week at a glance

  • How things are going. Appointments booked came in at 200, up from 191 the week before, with 23 marked as showed. Conversations slipped to 1,495 from 1,585 at $14.88 each, while lead forms rose to 417 from 369 and cost $53.36 each, down from $61.81.

  • What is next. We recommend testing the Corona ad's pitch in Surf City, since that ad brings conversations at $6.56 each against $19.14 in the Surf City message campaign, and holding Surf City's budget flat until the test has a full week of data.
  • Creative ideas to consider. All five top ads share one pitch: a VIP appointment, all credit types welcome, ITIN, own business, first buyer.
