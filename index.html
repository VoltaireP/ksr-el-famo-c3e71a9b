This week at a glance

  • How things are going. Lead forms rose to 809 from 408 the week before, and conversations rose to 1,834 from 1,481 at $14.60 each, down from $16.18. Appointments booked were 656, against 516 the week before on the same calendar list.
  • What to look into. Oxnard conversations cost $19.30 each, the highest of any dealership, and none of its 61 booked appointments are marked as showed. Worth checking that the Oxnard team marks shows in the CRM.
  • What is next. We recommend raising the Black Friday lead form budgets in Puente Hills and Surf City, where lead forms cost $5.55 and $8.41 this week, and checking how many of those leads book before raising more.
  • Creative ideas to consider. The top ads are Spanish videos and posts selling a VIP appointment at 5 or more dealerships. The Puente Hills video, 'Yes, help me get a car', brought 109 conversations. The Mazda video, 'Yes yes, I want a new car', for all credit types, brought 94 at $8.29 per result, the cheapest. The new Surf City post for buyers with great credit brought 65 in its first week. Worth testing that great credit version in the Mazda and Kia areas, where the top ads pitch all credit types.
