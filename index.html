This week at a glance

  • How things are going. Conversations rose to 1,621 from 1,438 the week before and cost $14.88 each, down from $15.92. Lead forms held at 351 against 341, at $68.70 each against $67.11.
  • What to look into. Appointments booked fell to 184 from 196 the week before, and only 5 are marked as showed against 14. Show marks for the last days of the week are likely still being entered, so worth checking before reading it as a drop.
  • What is next. We recommend moving part of the Surf City message budget, the biggest of any campaign at $21.00 per conversation, toward the Audi and Mazda message campaigns, which ran under $10 per conversation.
  • Creative ideas to consider. Most top ads use the same Spanish pitch: a VIP appointment at 5 or 7 dealerships, with ITIN, own business, first buyer and all credit welcome. The Puente Hills video, 'Yes, help me get a car', brought 88 conversations at $10.93, and the Mazda video, 'Yes yes, I want a new car', 57 at $8.27. The Corona post for buyers with excellent credit brought 69 at $9.09. Worth testing that good-credit angle in Surf City, where conversations cost $25.34.
