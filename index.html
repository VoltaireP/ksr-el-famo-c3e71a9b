This week at a glance

  • How things are going. Appointments booked rose to 243 from 202 the week before, and 50 appointments showed up. Conversations held steady at 1,585 for $14.39 each, while lead form sign-ups slipped to 369 from 404.

  • What is next. The new September videos went live across the dealerships between Sep 22 and Sep 26, so next we will give them a full week before judging them, then move budget toward the dealerships with the lowest cost per conversation, Mazda and Kia first.
  • Creative ideas to consider. The video ads that open with 'yes, help me get a car' and list who we help (ITIN, self-employed, first-time buyers, all credit types) start the most conversations. The work truck angle is the most efficient of the top ads. Next we will test it in the other regions.
